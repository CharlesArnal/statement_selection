I have gathered several mathematical textbooks, each under the format of a folder containing various tex files, pdfs and other files.
Our main goal is to extract every mathematical statement in each of these textbooks, and to assess whether it is already present in mathlib. I have written more detailed instructions at instructions.md.
I want you to read them thoroughly, then to execute them on the following textbooks, one after the other:
- mit_books/nonparametrics_and_robustness
- mit_books/number_theory_i
- mit_books/number_theory_ii_class_field_theory
- mit_books/probabilistic_methods_in_combinatorics
- mit_books/probabilistically_checkable_proofs
- mit_books/projection_theory
- mit_books/rational_points_on_elliptic_curves
