## Statement 1: Theorem 1.1 (Kronecker-Weber)
**Status**: not included
No formalization of the Kronecker-Weber theorem exists in mathlib v4.27.0. Mathlib has cyclotomic extensions (Mathlib/NumberTheory/Cyclotomic/) but does not prove that every abelian extension of Q is cyclotomic.

**Status**: not included
