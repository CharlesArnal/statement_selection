# Detailed Assessment: Theory of Probability Statements in Mathlib

## Statement 1: Theorem (Measures on R)
**Status**: included
**Explanation**: The Stieltjes measure construction from a right-continuous non-decreasing function is formalized in mathlib. The `StieltjesFunction` structure captures exactly such functions, and the associated measure is constructed via `StieltjesFunction.measure`.
**Mathlib references**: `Mathlib/MeasureTheory/Measure/Stieltjes.lean` (`StieltjesFunction`, `StieltjesFunction.measure`).

## Statement 2: Theorem (pi-lambda theorem)
**Status**: included
**Explanation**: Dynkin's pi-lambda theorem is formalized in mathlib as `induction_on_inter`. The file defines `IsPiSystem` and `DynkinSystem` and proves that if a lambda-system contains a pi-system, it contains the sigma-algebra generated by the pi-system.
**Mathlib references**: `Mathlib/MeasureTheory/PiSystem.lean` (`IsPiSystem`, `DynkinSystem`, `induction_on_inter`).

## Statement 3: Theorem (Caratheodory Extension Theorem)
**Status**: included
**Explanation**: The Caratheodory extension theorem is formalized in mathlib. The outer measure Caratheodory construction and the extension from algebras to sigma-algebras are both present.
**Mathlib references**: `Mathlib/MeasureTheory/OuterMeasure/Caratheodory.lean`, `Mathlib/MeasureTheory/OuterMeasure/OfAddContent.lean`.

## Statement 4: Theorem (Measures on R^d)
**Status**: included
**Explanation**: The product measure construction for finite products of sigma-finite measure spaces is formalized via `MeasureTheory.Measure.pi`. This gives the unique measure on product spaces with the desired property on rectangles.
**Mathlib references**: `Mathlib/MeasureTheory/Constructions/Pi.lean` (`MeasureTheory.Measure.pi`).

## Statement 5: Theorem (Measurability via generators)
**Status**: included
**Explanation**: The result that a function is measurable if its preimages of generating sets are measurable is formalized as `measurable_generateFrom`.
**Mathlib references**: `Mathlib/MeasureTheory/MeasurableSpace/Basic.lean` (`measurable_generateFrom`).

## Statement 6: Theorem (Properties of integration)
**Status**: included
**Explanation**: All five properties of integration listed are formalized in mathlib: nonnegativity (`integral_nonneg`), linearity (`integral_add`, `integral_smul`), monotonicity (`integral_mono`), a.e.-equality invariance (`integral_congr_ae`), and the triangle inequality (`norm_integral_le_integral_norm`).
**Mathlib references**: `Mathlib/MeasureTheory/Integral/Bochner/Basic.lean` (`integral_nonneg`, `integral_add`, `integral_smul`, `integral_mono`, `integral_congr_ae`, `norm_integral_le_integral_norm`).

## Statement 7: Jensen's inequality
**Status**: included
**Explanation**: Jensen's inequality for integrals is formalized in mathlib. For probability measures, `ConvexOn.map_average_le` gives that a convex function applied to the average is at most the average of the convex function.
**Mathlib references**: `Mathlib/Analysis/Convex/Integral.lean` (`ConvexOn.map_average_le`, `ConvexOn.map_integral_le`).

## Statement 8: Holder's inequality
**Status**: included
**Explanation**: Holder's inequality for integrals is formalized for both `ENNReal` and `NNReal` functions. The statement `ENNReal.lintegral_mul_le_Lp_mul_Lq` gives the standard Holder inequality for conjugate exponents.
**Mathlib references**: `Mathlib/MeasureTheory/Integral/MeanInequalities.lean` (`ENNReal.lintegral_mul_le_Lp_mul_Lq`, `NNReal.lintegral_mul_le_Lp_mul_Lq`).

## Statement 9: Cauchy-Schwarz inequality
**Status**: included
**Explanation**: The Cauchy-Schwarz inequality is formalized both as `norm_inner_le_norm` in the inner product space setting and as a special case of Holder's inequality with p = q = 2.
**Mathlib references**: `Mathlib/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Defs.lean` (`norm_inner_le_norm`), `Mathlib/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Basic.lean`.

## Statement 10: Bounded convergence theorem
**Status**: included
**Explanation**: The bounded convergence theorem is a special case of the dominated convergence theorem (with the dominating function being the constant bound). The dominated convergence theorem `tendsto_integral_of_dominated_convergence` is formalized in mathlib and subsumes the bounded convergence theorem.
**Mathlib references**: `Mathlib/MeasureTheory/Integral/DominatedConvergence.lean` (`tendsto_integral_of_dominated_convergence`).

## Statement 11: Fatou's lemma
**Status**: included
**Explanation**: Fatou's lemma is formalized as `lintegral_liminf_le` which states that the integral of the liminf is at most the liminf of the integrals.
**Mathlib references**: `Mathlib/MeasureTheory/Integral/Lebesgue/Add.lean` (`lintegral_liminf_le`, `lintegral_liminf_le'`).

## Statement 12: Monotone convergence theorem
**Status**: included
**Explanation**: The monotone convergence theorem is formalized as `lintegral_iSup` which states that for a monotone sequence of non-negative measurable functions, the integral of the supremum equals the supremum of the integrals.
**Mathlib references**: `Mathlib/MeasureTheory/Integral/Lebesgue/Add.lean` (`lintegral_iSup`).

## Statement 13: Dominated convergence theorem
**Status**: included
**Explanation**: The dominated convergence theorem is formalized in mathlib in both the Lebesgue integral and Bochner integral settings.
**Mathlib references**: `Mathlib/MeasureTheory/Integral/DominatedConvergence.lean` (`tendsto_integral_of_dominated_convergence`), `Mathlib/MeasureTheory/Integral/Lebesgue/DominatedConvergence.lean`.

## Statement 14: Theorem (Strong law of large numbers)
**Status**: included
**Explanation**: The strong law of large numbers is formalized as `ProbabilityTheory.strong_law_ae`. It proves that for i.i.d. integrable random variables, the empirical mean converges almost surely to the expectation. The proof follows Etemadi's approach and only requires pairwise independence.
**Mathlib references**: `Mathlib/Probability/StrongLaw.lean` (`ProbabilityTheory.strong_law_ae`, `ProbabilityTheory.strong_law_ae_real`).

## Statement 15: Theorem (Independence and pi-systems)
**Status**: included
**Explanation**: The result that independence of pi-systems implies independence of generated sigma-algebras is formalized as `IndepSets.indep`. This is a key structural result for independence in probability theory.
**Mathlib references**: `Mathlib/Probability/Independence/Basic.lean` (`IndepSets.indep`, `iIndepSets.indepSets`).

## Statement 16: Theorem (Standard Borel spaces)
**Status**: included
**Explanation**: Standard Borel spaces are formalized via the `StandardBorelSpace` typeclass. The result that uncountable standard Borel spaces are measurably isomorphic to the reals is proved as `PolishSpace.measurableEquivOfNotCountable`. The embedding into reals is also established.
**Mathlib references**: `Mathlib/MeasureTheory/Constructions/Polish/Basic.lean` (`StandardBorelSpace`, `PolishSpace.measurableEquivOfNotCountable`), `Mathlib/MeasureTheory/Constructions/Polish/EmbeddingReal.lean` (`exists_subset_real_measurableEquiv`).

## Statement 17: Fubini's theorem
**Status**: included
**Explanation**: Fubini's theorem for the product of two sigma-finite measure spaces is formalized. The product measure is constructed and the iterated integral equals the product integral.
**Mathlib references**: `Mathlib/MeasureTheory/Integral/Prod.lean` (`integral_prod`, Fubini's theorem), `Mathlib/MeasureTheory/Measure/Prod.lean` (product measure construction).

## Statement 18: Markov's inequality
**Status**: included
**Explanation**: Markov's inequality is formalized as `mul_meas_ge_le_lintegral` which states that for a non-negative measurable function, `epsilon * mu({f >= epsilon}) <= integral(f)`.
**Mathlib references**: `Mathlib/MeasureTheory/Integral/Lebesgue/Markov.lean` (`mul_meas_ge_le_lintegral`, `mul_meas_ge_le_lintegral₀`).

## Statement 19: Chebyshev's inequality
**Status**: included
**Explanation**: Chebyshev's inequality is formalized as `ProbabilityTheory.meas_ge_le_variance_div_sq`, which states that for a random variable with finite variance, `P(|X - mu| >= k) <= sigma^2 / k^2`.
**Mathlib references**: `Mathlib/Probability/Moments/Variance.lean` (`ProbabilityTheory.meas_ge_le_variance_div_sq`, `ProbabilityTheory.meas_ge_le_evariance_div_sq`).
