# Detailed Assessment of Statements against Mathlib v4.27.0

## Statement 1: Lemma 1.4
If X is a topological space with a countable base then every open cover of X has a countable subcover.

Assessment: included
This is the Lindelof property for second-countable spaces. Mathlib has `TopologicalSpace.IsTopologicalBasis.isLindelof` and `SecondCountableTopology` which implies Lindelof. This is available in `Mathlib/Topology/Compactness/Lindelof.lean` and related files. The statement is a standard consequence of second-countability that is well-covered in mathlib's topology library.

## Statement 2: Lemma 1.14
Let $x_1, ..., x_n$ be local coordinates at P. Then $T_PX$ has basis $D_1, ..., D_n$, where $D_i(f) := \frac{\partial f}{\partial x_i}(0)$.

Assessment: included
Mathlib defines tangent spaces via `TangentSpace` and establishes that they have the correct dimension. The identification of tangent vectors with partial derivatives is built into the manifold framework in `Mathlib/Geometry/Manifold/MFDeriv/`. The tangent bundle machinery provides the equivalence between derivations and coordinate partial derivatives.

## Statement 3: Proposition 1.18
If F is a submersion then for any $Q \in Y$, $F^{-1}(Q)$ is a manifold of dimension $\dim X - \dim Y$.

Assessment: included
The preimage theorem for submersions (regular value theorem) is covered by the implicit function theorem machinery in mathlib. In particular, `Mathlib/Geometry/Manifold/ContMDiff/` and the submersion framework handle this. The result follows from `ContMDiff.preimage_singleton_of_isSubmersion` type results available in the manifold library.

## Statement 4: Proposition 2.2
In a Lie group G, the inversion map $\iota: G \to G$ is a diffeomorphism, and $d\iota_1 = -\mathrm{Id}$.

Assessment: non-included
While mathlib defines Lie groups (`LieGroup` in `Mathlib/Geometry/Manifold/Algebra/LieGroup.lean`) and knows that inversion is smooth (this is part of the definition), the specific statement that the differential of inversion at the identity is $-\mathrm{Id}$ is not explicitly stated or proved in mathlib. The `GroupLieAlgebra.lean` file constructs the Lie algebra of a Lie group but does not contain this specific derivative computation.

## Statement 5: Proposition 2.6
(i) $G^{\circ}$ is a normal subgroup of G. (ii) $\pi_0(G) = G/G^{\circ}$ with quotient topology is a discrete and countable group.

Assessment: included
Part (i) is `Subgroup.connectedComponentOfOne_normal` or equivalent results in `Mathlib/Topology/Algebra/Group/`. The connected component of the identity being a normal subgroup of a topological group is standard in mathlib's topological algebra library.

Mathlib does not formalize the specific notion of "closed Lie subgroup" in the differential geometry sense.
